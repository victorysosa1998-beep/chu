YOUR FIRST STEPS
WITH US

01

Just Show Up

Come as you are — no dress code, no prior church experience needed. Walk through our doors and be welcomed like family. Our ushers will guide you every step of the way.

02

Encounter the Word

Sit in and experience life-transforming teaching from God's Word. Every service is designed to meet you exactly where you are and propel you forward in purpose.

03

Connect with Us

After service, meet our pastors and volunteers. Let us know it's your first time — we'd love to personally welcome you and help you find your place in the family.

04

Join a Group

Get rooted through our discipleship programs and small groups. Growth happens in community — and we have the perfect next step waiting for you.

05

Get Baptized

Water baptism is your public declaration of faith. We hold regular baptism services and would love to celebrate this milestone with you and your loved ones.

06

Serve & Impact

Discover your gifts and use them for the Kingdom. From worship to evangelism, there's a place in this ministry where you can burn brightly for God.

Our Foundation

WHAT WE
BELIEVE

The Bible is God's Word

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our ultimate authority for faith and daily living.

Salvation Through Christ Alone

Jesus Christ, God's Son, died for our sins and rose again. Salvation is through faith in Him — it's a free gift, not earned.

The Holy Spirit is Active Today

We believe in the baptism and gifts of the Holy Spirit for every believer — healing, miracles, and divine power are for today.

The Church is God's Family

The local church is God's plan to disciple, care for, and deploy believers into their God-given destiny and purpose.

God Heals and Delivers

Divine healing is part of the redemptive work of Christ. We believe God still heals bodies, minds, and broken lives today.

Every Life Has a Destiny

God has a specific plan for every person. Our mission is to help you discover, develop, and deploy your God-given destiny.

THE PRAYER
THAT CHANGES
EVERYTHING

If you've never given your heart to Jesus, or you want to recommit your life to Him — say this prayer with us right now. It's the most important decision you'll ever make.

"Lord Jesus, I believe You are the Son of God. I believe You died for my sins and rose again. I confess my sins and ask You to forgive me. I invite You into my heart as my Lord and Saviour. From today, I belong to You. Fill me with Your Holy Spirit and lead me into the fullness of Your plan for my life. Thank You for saving me.

Amen.

If you just prayed that prayer — congratulations! You are now a child of God. We would love to walk this journey with you. Please reach out to us so we can celebrate with you and connect you to a church family.
