About City of Champion Fire

Welcome to the official website of City of Champions International Assembly. It is always a joy to connect with you and welcome you into our church family. City of Champions International Assembly is a dynamic, people-focused ministry committed to restoring human destiny and dignity through the transforming power of God. We are passionate about teaching the Word of God with clarity, simplicity, and power so that lives can be changed and purpose can be discovered.

Under the leadership of our Senior Pastor, the ministry continues to witness lives being transformed, hope restored, and faith strengthened. Our mission is to raise a generation of champions who will impact and dominate their world through the fire and power of the Holy Spirit. We invite you to take a step of faith and discover all that God has prepared for you. We believe that when you walk with God, your life cannot remain the same
